# Painted character animation

The Living Steppe Tapestry character sheets contain 48 painted poses: four locomotion frames and two attack frames for each of eight character designs. They were generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using `public/art/steppe-actors.png` as a character-design and painting-style reference. The original illustration remains available for UI artwork.

| Sheet     | Characters                                            | Local files                                                                       |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mounted   | Commander, allied archer, wolf, ghost rider           | `public/art/steppe-mounted-motion.png`, `public/art/steppe-mounted-motion.json`   |
| Creatures | Winged shade, earth creature, hollow seer, Skybreaker | `public/art/steppe-creature-motion.png`, `public/art/steppe-creature-motion.json` |

Each PNG is 1536 × 1024 with transparent alpha. Metadata crops the individual silhouettes without modifying or slicing the source PNG. The generated row spacing varies slightly, so the atlas uses explicit frame rectangles rather than assuming a uniform spritesheet grid.

Every trimmed frame registers to a **256 × 360 logical canvas**, with feet or the bottom of the hovering silhouette at **y = 324**. The common 0.5, 0.9 origin keeps the same world anchor through frame changes. The source canvas, visual scale, collision radius, and gameplay position are independent of the painted crop.

`src/game/animation/CharacterMotion.ts` is renderer-independent. It advances only when supplied active gameplay seconds, selects grounded idle or four-frame locomotion according to movement, increases gait cadence for faster riding, and selects attack windup/release poses. Hovering shades and seers have slow idle loops. Stable per-actor offsets prevent synchronized marching. Reset clears all pose and hit-feedback state when an enemy is reused from its pool.

## Mounted-sheet generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production animation sprite sheet for the painterly Mongolian-inspired game Hordes of the Fallen Sky. The supplied image is a CHARACTER DESIGN AND PAINTING STYLE REFERENCE, not an edit target; use only its mounted commander (top left), mounted archer (top row second), wolf (top row third), and ghost rider (bottom row second). Create a NEW genuinely transparent sheet. EXACTLY SIX equal columns by FOUR equal rows, 24 complete isolated sprites. Landscape canvas ideally 1536x1024, uniform rectangular cells, no drawn grid or labels. All characters face RIGHT in the same high three-quarter battlefield view. Each row is the SAME character repeated in six animation poses at an IDENTICAL camera, scale, ground registration, and body center. Every subject fits in the central 86% of its cell with transparent gutters; tails, bows, wings and weapons must not enter neighboring cells. Ground-contact line is 84% of each cell's height, body center x=50%. No background, no baked floor, no shadow, no dust.
> Row 1: the lapis-blue Mongolian mounted commander on chestnut horse from the reference, small blue shoulder pennant, bow.
> Row 2: the rust-red Mongolian mounted archer on dark bay horse from the reference.
> Row 3: the lean charcoal supernatural wolf with ivory angular mane and faint amber eyes from the reference.
> Row 4: the spectral mounted rider on a blue-grey spirit horse, tattered ivory and indigo cloth from the reference.
> For EACH row, columns 1–4 are FOUR DISTINCT successive evenly spaced phases of a readable looping canter/run: contact with forelegs reaching, compression with legs gathering, lifted passing phase, and extension with hindlegs pushing. Actual leg articulation; do not simply translate or rotate the same pose. Horse/rider torsos and center stay aligned, only gentle natural saddle movement, tails and cloth trail differently. Wolf has distinct four-legged run phases. Columns 5–6 are an ATTACK pair: for mounted characters draw bow then release arrow with the bow arm extended; for wolf crouch/snarl then lunge with jaws open. Do not include a free flying arrow projectile. All six poses must keep the same body scale and ground registration.
> Match the original miniature painterly 2.5D assets closely: matte gouache, strong readable silhouette, warm ivory, ochre, lapis turquoise, rust, umber and slate blue, simplified painted details, soft brush grain, light from upper left. Original fantasy artwork with Mongolian and Central Asian textile decoration. Not pixel art, not glossy 3D, not a photo. No text, letters, numbers, cell borders, watermark, extra characters or props. Keep all 24 complete subjects clearly separated on real transparent alpha.

## Creature-sheet generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent production animation sprite sheet for the painterly Mongolian-inspired fantasy game Hordes of the Fallen Sky. The attached image is a CHARACTER DESIGN AND PAINTING STYLE REFERENCE, not an edit target. Create a NEW sheet using its winged shade (top right), earth creature (bottom left), hollow seer (bottom row third), and antlered Skybreaker (bottom right). EXACTLY SIX equal columns by FOUR equal rows, 24 isolated complete full-body sprites on GENUINELY TRANSPARENT alpha. Landscape canvas ideally 1536x1024; uniform cells with consistent camera, character scale, and ground registration. No cell borders, letters, text or labels. All subjects face RIGHT or three-quarter right, light upper left. Each subject stays inside the central 86% of its cell, including every wingtip, branch and ribbon, with clear transparent gutters. Ground contact is 84% of each cell height, x-center 50%. Body center stays fixed across each row. No background, floor, ground shadows, dust, or disconnected effects.
> Row 1: the unsettling winged wraith, smoky indigo cloth wings and bone-white empty face.
> Row 2: the hulking ochre rock-and-grass earth creature, layered stone and hollow turquoise-lit face.
> Row 3: the floating hollow seer, indigo robes, ivory mask, branch-like staff with a small restrained turquoise spirit light.
> Row 4: the enormous gaunt antlered Skybreaker, horse-skull face, ash-grey body, tattered indigo drapes, faint turquoise ribs; show its entire antler span and feet, never crop it.
> For each row, columns 1–4 are FOUR DISTINCT evenly spaced phases of a LOOPING motion. Shade: wings high, folding down, wings low, raising again, cloth tips actually change silhouette. Earth creature: left-foot contact, heavy compression, right-foot contact, pushing off, with articulated stone limbs. Seer: subtle hovering robe flutter and staff sway, clear changing cloth silhouettes, same face and scale. Skybreaker: weighty alternating step and breath, articulated leg positions and slight head/antler motion, consistent size. Columns 5–6 are an ATTACK pair: shade folds wings then lunges with wings spread; earth creature raises a heavy fist then punches; seer raises staff then points it forward to release a spell; Skybreaker raises its skull and antlers then lowers its skull and spreads its chest to cast an ominous blast. Keep attack silhouettes inside the cell. Do not paint separate projectiles.
> Match the original painterly miniature 2.5D style closely: matte gouache brushwork, readable elegant simplified silhouettes, ochre and umber rock, cold slate/lapis indigo cloth, bone ivory, subdued turquoise spirit light. Original unsettling fantasy, no gore. Not pixel art, not glossy 3D, not photoreal. All six poses of each creature must be recognizably the SAME creature, same viewpoint, same proportions and scale, positioned consistently for frame-by-frame animation. Do not simply copy and rotate one pose. Exactly 24 full bodies, clean transparent alpha around every sprite.
