# Combat readability

Elite arrivals carry a brass ground ring and **ELITE** label with a small health track. Armoured bulwarks use **ARMOURED** so their role stays distinct. The existing boss bar describes Skybreaker chapter, warning time, and vulnerability. Enemy markers are bounded to six visible actors; bosses retain their dedicated bar. Pooled enemy flags reset on reuse and preserve elite identity in full saves, including dynamically selected local champions.

Offscreen incoming projectiles, charging windups, ranged releases and Skybreaker warnings receive directional edge labels. Projectile prediction uses actual world velocity and collision targets, ignores moving-away and safely passing shots, and considers the next four seconds. Positions project through the active 2D or 2.5D camera. Eight direction sectors group volleys; at most four imminent warnings occupy the HUD. Labels distinguish threats to the Khan from fire heading toward an offscreen camp. These are pointer-transparent and hide in menus.

Major clan loss messages retain cause, source when known, resolve damage, casualties and a practical response for twelve active seconds. Small later hits do not immediately overwrite the explanation. Starvation, ground eruptions, seer attacks and local spells have distinct advice. Both the latest hit and latest major loss survive expedition saves; prior saves can omit the additional fields.

Settings now includes **Projectile contrast** (Standard/High) and **Effect density** (Full/Reduced/Minimal). High contrast makes hostile projectiles bright amber and larger visually, with a light/dark outline, while retaining their world collision radius and damage. Density reduces dust, impact motes, defeat fades and shake. Minimal removes these decorative effects; attack telegraphs, enemy/projectile bodies, warning markers and damage simulation remain intact. Settings apply to live and restored projectiles and persist through the normal validated native profile framework. They are profile preferences, independent of expedition slots. No replacement artwork or rendering architecture is introduced.

Primary modules: `shared/visualSettings.ts`, `systems/combatReadability.ts`, `ui/combatReadabilityContent.ts`, and the scene's existing actor/effect hooks. Unit tests cover trajectory prediction, grouping, limits, escaping, major-loss retention and preference compatibility. Browser tests cover persistence, unchanged hit areas/damage, minimal decorative effects and retained danger/elite feedback.
