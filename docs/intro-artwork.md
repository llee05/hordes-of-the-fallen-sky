# Intro story paintings

The three prologue illustrations were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The selected PNGs are bundled unchanged in `public/art/`, at 1536 × 1024 each. There are no runtime image downloads or AI calls. `src/game/data/intro.ts` pairs each painting with its chapter and caption.

## Prompt set

Each request used this shared direction:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-bleed landscape opening-story illustration for the offline desktop game Hordes of the Fallen Sky. Style: The Living Steppe Tapestry, matte hand-painted gouache, restrained brush grain, simplified elegant miniature silhouettes, high three-quarter landscape view. Thronefall’s miniature readability, The Banner Saga’s illustrated surfaces, traditional Mongolian/Central Asian felt appliqué and woven geometry. Original fantasy painting. Cinematic wide landscape, ideally 1536 by 1024. Keep important subjects in the central upper two thirds and a calm lower quarter for a separately rendered caption. No text, captions, typography, logos, borders, watermark, photorealism, glossy 3D, or pixel art.

### When the grass was gold — `public/art/intro-steppe.png`

> First panel of a three-scene prologue, “When the grass was gold”. A peaceful Mongolian-inspired nomadic camp in a vast golden ochre and sage steppe, traditional ivory felt gers with turquoise/rust woven bands, decorated wooden carts, grazing chestnut horses, tiny mounted riders, turquoise and vermilion banners. Blue sky opens above distant rolling hills, warm light from upper left, soft olive shadows. Calm lower quarter of grass for a separately rendered caption. Palette warm ochre, ivory, muted lapis turquoise, rust red, olive, umber.

### Then heaven fell silent — `public/art/intro-fallen-sky.png`

> Second panel of a three-scene prologue, “Then heaven fell silent”. Across Mongolian-inspired golden grasslands a vast unnatural indigo storm rolls toward a distant abandoned walled settlement, cloud fissures glimmer with restrained cold turquoise light. In the middle distance unsettling tiny ash-grey spectral horsemen and angular indigo wraith silhouettes emerge from the storm; no close monsters. A few ivory gers and a fallen turquoise banner stand on the deserted nearer grass. Ominous but elegant, no gore, no explosions. Low horizon beneath sweeping sculpted storm clouds, calm darker lower quarter for separately rendered caption. Ochre grass and umber earth underneath lapis-indigo, ash and bone; restrained turquoise supernatural light.

### Carry the blue banner — `public/art/intro-caravan.png`

> Third panel of a three-scene prologue, “Carry the blue banner”. A small Mongolian-inspired nomadic clan heads east through golden steppe toward a distant sacred mountain passage glowing warm ivory in dawn light. A mounted commander in lapis-blue deel and simple lamellar armor rides a chestnut horse with a turquoise pennant; a handful of tiny families and decorated felt-covered wooden carts follow, leading spare horses. Turquoise and rust-red banners trail behind. The indigo supernatural storm is far behind to left, hope ahead. Caravan readable in middle band, mountain centered beyond, calm darker foreground grass in lower quarter for separate caption. Ochre gold and sage grasses, ivory felt, muted lapis turquoise, rust, umber and olive ground shadows, warm upper-left light breaking through cloud.
