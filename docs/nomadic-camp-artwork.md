# Nomadic camp painting

The camp uses two original PNGs made with the built-in `image_gen` tool. All art is bundled locally; the game never generates or downloads assets at runtime.

| Asset                                   | Size              | Use                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `public/art/nomadic-camp-terrain.png`   | 1774 × 887        | Continuous grassland panorama, paths, low banks, shrubs and rocks       |
| `public/art/nomadic-camp-buildings.png` | 1254 × 1254, RGBA | Three successive stages each of ger, wagon workplace and archery ground |

The building sheet uses `public/art/steppe-props.png` as a painting and material reference. The generated PNGs are preserved unchanged. Explicit frame rectangles in `nomadic-camp-buildings.json` clip the nine silhouettes in the shared SVG atlas renderer in `src/ui/artwork.ts`. Metadata ignores near-transparent gutter noise when establishing frame bounds; it does not modify the bitmap. The registry still owns level, scale and ornaments. Other building types reuse existing sheep, pack horses, cairns and banners. Interface shadows provide ground contact without changing map anchors, click targets, plaques or game state.

The panorama fills the existing map bounds and supports compact panning. It has no built-in structures or text, so optional ownership remains visible and accurate. Artwork stays still while planning; there are no new clocks or animations.

More distinct portable-forge, healer, hunting, storyteller and training-equipment designs would further distinguish workplaces that currently share architecture. This milestone supplies the terrain and three-stage architecture families rather than inventing new gameplay or a separate management interface.

## Terrain generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background painting for the existing flat 2D Nomadic Camp strategy-game panel. Create an original, polished hand-painted steppe terrain panorama, landscape 2:1 composition, ideally 1536 by 768. It is an overhead illustrated manuscript map looking down over gently rolling grassland, no horizon, no tilted island, no buildings, no people, no text or interface. This is a QUIET BACKGROUND under ten clickable building locations; keep the central area readable. Matte gouache with restrained brush grain and beautiful small natural variations: sage and golden ochre grasses, dusty olive earth, small tufts, scattered muted shrubs and stones near the outside edges, subtle terraced grassy banks, warm upper-left sunlight and soft olive atmospheric shadows. A worn network of narrow pale tan footpaths joins these exact map coordinates (percent from left, top): center 50,31; 19,28; 16,64; 82,28;84,64;34,12;66,12;38,60;62,60;50,82. Let paths curve naturally between these small quiet clearings. Do not draw circles, badges or artificial boundaries at those locations. Terrain is a painted continuous world, not a map with gridlines. Warm ivory, muted ochre, sage, olive and umber, restrained lapis accents in small flowers only. Consistent with miniature Mongolian and Central Asian fantasy artwork, premium medieval manuscript strategy game, original assets only. Detailed near edges, quiet inside, soft directional shadows grounded in terrain, no large trees blocking locations. No sky, no mountains in horizon, no snow, no river, no bright saturation, no 3D render, no photorealism, no lettering, no watermark, no border.

## Building atlas generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: NEW transparent production building atlas for the flat painted Nomadic Camp interface in Hordes of the Fallen Sky. The supplied existing props are STYLE AND MATERIAL REFERENCES ONLY; do not edit that image. Generate exactly NINE isolated small building vignettes in THREE equal columns by THREE equal rows, square 1536x1536 sheet, truly transparent alpha between objects, no background, no ground patches, no baked shadows, no grid or labels. Every vignette fits inside the central 82% of its 512x512 cell, with generous transparent gutters. Consistent high three-quarter overhead viewpoint and upper-left warm light, matte painted gouache, detailed ivory felt, weathered timber, turquoise and rust textile, restrained brass trim. Readable miniature architecture with original Central Asian geometric ornament. Row1 SAME Khan's ger in three successive upgrades: col1 simple functional round ivory ger with wooden door; col2 larger ger with elaborate turquoise/rust doorway, woven bands and two low supply chests; col3 grand dignified wide ger with layered entrance canopy, rich lapis/rust woven trim, small brass finial, two side storage chests. No stone castle or Chinese palace. Row2 SAME wagon workplace in three upgrades: col1 a single practical covered two-wheel supply cart; col2 covered cart with workbench, neatly tied timber and extra storage; col3 impressive pair of supply carts, carved wooden workbench and decorated canopy, tied stores, highly legible. Row3 SAME nomadic archery training ground in three upgrades: col1 two straw practice targets on wooden stands and a bow rack; col2 three targets and a small fabric-covered weapons rack; col3 impressive decorated timber practice gate with turquoise cloth canopy, straw targets and bows stored at either side. NO RIDERS OR PEOPLE. Within each row center all stages similarly and keep full silhouettes inside each cell. Higher levels should be visibly more developed, but stay portable nomadic structures. Painting should match the reference's ivory, turquoise, vermilion and warm weathered wood, with calm shadows on the objects themselves and crisp transparent edges. No photorealism, no glossy 3D, no smoke, no lettering, numbers, watermark, emblems copied from another game, terrain or cast shadow outside structures.
