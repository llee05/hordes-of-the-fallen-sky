# Research V2 effect delivery status

All 166 IDs are registered. 118 technologies have runtime effects; 48 proposed effects remain unavailable.
Some working effects have unavailable prerequisites and cannot yet be purchased in play. Catalogue
reachability tests ignore only runtime availability; they do not enable these effects.
A fresh expedition resets all research; full expedition saves retain purchases.

No proposed effect has been substituted with a stat bonus. Wagon defence alone has
the documented zero-building guard-shot adaptation. No respec is offered.

Lus implementation defaults where the design has no number: Undertow pulls 50 paces;
Geomancer grants 10% speed for 2 seconds (8 second cooldown); ridge patches use a
70 pace radius and 20% slow; rock arcs hit within 140 paces for 120% shared damage;
Land in Motion heals 0.2 Khan health/s in water and deals 15% shared damage/s in stone.
Golomt defaults: Ash-Born pushes 75 paces within 140; fire pulses deal 120% shared
damage within 150 at one-second intervals; Eternal Hearth heals 0.5 Khan health/s,
restores 0.15 resolve/s, and pushes normal foes 28 paces/s within 160.

Infinite Remembrance defaults to ten pickups with gaps below four active seconds,
then four seconds of 500-pace shard attraction and +20% echo damage. Bonus shards
carry a nonrecursive flag through saves; pool exhaustion preserves their XP.

Banner Formation reuses F / Change formation to select Ring (guard), Wedge, or
Column (retreat), and remains selectable each crossing. Defaults: Wedge +10% rider
damage; Ring 10% clan protection; Column +10% migration mobility. Great Kurultai
is issued with R or the Rally host button, once per crossing. Roadward Captains
remains unavailable because there are no individual wagon hit events or escort assignments.

Guardian defaults: Arrow Platforms uses a 220-pace, 60%-damage guard shot every six
seconds; ricochets seek a normal foe within 180 paces and cannot ricochet again.
City of Felt and Wood covers up to 40 materials after other cost modifiers.
Fast Unpacking applies only after a completed crossing; both first-construction
benefits use the existing purchase transaction and never consume on preview.

Of the 67 formerly deferred effects, 65 are now connected. Two remain unavailable:
Roadward Captains requires individual wagon hit events and rider escort assignments;
Bounty in Scarcity requires real reserve capacity to evaluate its 25%/40% thresholds.
Absolute food amounts are not substituted for those percentages.

The following working effects still have unavailable ancestors; tests grant ownership
directly to execute their runtime mechanics: Call of the Pack, Endless Pursuit,
Camp of Craftspeople, The Wandering City, Unbroken Seasons, Horn of the Host,
Banner Formation, The Last Wagon, The Great Kurultai, Defended Passage, Fast Unpacking,
City of Felt and Wood, Living Waters, Thousand Arrows, Last Stand of the Hearth.
The purchase validator retains every prerequisite and rejects these purchases until
the corresponding discovery, camp, provisions, wagon, or structure systems land.

Hybrid defaults: Ghost-Fletched arrows deal 35% echo damage and inherit piercing
first, otherwise fire arrows, with no extra projectile-count modifier. Living Waters
requires three near-camp kills in the current wave, restores 2 pasture and 2 food,
and has a 20-second cooldown. Thousand Arrows supports from riders within 400 paces.
Last Stand accelerates the ward clock for eight seconds on crossing below 20% resolve;
remaining below the threshold cannot retrigger it. Blood and Ember uses the shared
healing path, including healing penalties and no revival during succession.

Shared runtime hooks remain available to later system work: `researchRuntime.ts`
owns Marked and the crossing material-spend ledger; `researchTerrain.ts` owns
per-spawn slow/root history; `researchCompanions.ts` selects the nearest companion
and releases echoes; `researchLeadership.ts` owns Banner Formation and rider
release clocks. All clocks use active seconds, and their state is retained by
`expeditionArchive.ts`. Rider/echo attack speed affects cadence rather than damage.

Warm Ashes is checked at the encounter recovery transition and grants no healing
while enemies, hostile projectiles, seer warnings, or lingering damaging enemy
area attacks remain active. Crossing-arrival healing,
including camp bonuses, follows Fortress of Felt and Household Oath rules.
Geomancer's Eye and Warning Signs require entering a warning area and safely
leaving it, or crossing it under Eye of the Mountain's Gallop protection.
Normal allied piercing arrows receive Drilling Shafts and World-Piercer bonuses
and penalties together; Ghost-Fletched arrows retain only their selected modifier.
Echo releases use per-echo active-time clocks; legacy ancestor timers remain readable.

Shifting Ridge places its patch only when an eruption resolves outside the Khan,
regardless of distance; protected warning crossings do not place patches early.
Stone and Stream applies to Gallop, Returning Current, Ash-Born and Eternal Hearth,
including lingering slows and slowing ground patches, without displacing bosses.
Crossing previews and migration shortage forecasts include Wandering City production.
Overlapping hoof trails retain a separate damage claim for each active dash and enemy spawn.
